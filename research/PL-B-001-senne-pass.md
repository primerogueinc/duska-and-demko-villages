It ran web searches and read pages; this agent then turned its report into the entries of batch PL-B-001. The report itself is in the owner's chat and is not reproduced here. ## Conflicts between sources

1. When "Ungszenna" began: 1904 (the law and the county weekly) or 1907 (first printed gazetteer). Both are probably right.
2. First mention of the parish: 1317 and 1319 (municipal history) or 1322 (Catholic lexicon).
3. Return of the church: 1718, with "1817" once on the same municipal page, which reads as a slip.
4. The manor: a 16th-century Dobó curia, or built in the first half of the 17th century on older foundations. Compatible.
5. Order of the Vécsey and Csápy families as owners: reversed between the municipal history and Hungarian Wikipedia.
6. Iňačovce as a Roman Catholic filial of Senné: listed in 1734, 1804, 1978 and today; missing in 1917.
7. Earliest Roman Catholic register: 1778 (municipal history), 1787 (a film list), possibly 1709 (an index page whose layout was ambiguous).
8. Where the 1869 sheets are: "Uzhhorod" in one source, the Berehove branch in another. The branch belongs to the same archive.
9. Vysoká nad Uhom in 1939: taken by Hungarian troops (Senné municipal history) or on the Slovak side of the line (the protocol). Read here as taken in the fighting and then left outside the ceded strip.
10. Roskoványi's birthday: 7 December 1807 in the biographical lexicons, 6 December in the Catholic lexicon.
11. Area of the Senné fishponds: 425 hectares for the Ramsar site, a larger figure elsewhere for the whole pond complex.
