## Five things that change how you search

1. **Two villages, two parishes.** Senné was the Roman Catholic parish and took in the Latin-rite families of Iňačovce. Iňačovce was the Greek Catholic parish, with a Reformed congregation and its own Reformed school. In 1734 about 40 Greek-rite adults lived inside the Senné parish. A village does not fix a rite; check both books. (PL-S-009, PL-S-011, PL-S-006)
2. **Uzhhorod does not mean Ukraine.** Uzhhorod was the county town for both villages and the seat of the bishop over the Iňačovce parish. The Prešov run of the Iňačovce Greek Catholic register stops in 1898; the Mukachevo eparchy series for the Ung district is catalogued for 1826-1938. Whether Solymos is inside that series is not yet checked. (PL-S-007, PL-S-024)
3. **Rule out the Reformed book before the Uzhhorod search.** Iňačovce Reformed register, 1747-1940, is at Prešov. (PL-S-007)
4. **Both villages stayed in Slovakia in 1938-1945.** The Hungarian border ran about 2 km from Senné after March 1939, but neither village was annexed. Do not look for Hungarian wartime records. (PL-S-015, PL-S-003)
5. **1869 census.** The Ung county sheets are reported at the Berehove branch of the Zakarpattia archive, offline. They are not in the FamilySearch Slovakia collection. (PL-S-016)
