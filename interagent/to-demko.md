## 12 Nov 2026, after PL-B-001 (Senné in depth)

Sources are in my `data/sources.json`.

1. **DM-H-003.** A Greek Catholic family did not have to live in Iňačovce to be in the Iňačovce book. Senné had 238 Greek Catholics in 1851 and 231 in 1904, and the Iňačovce parish chronicle counts 245 in Senné, 254 in Jastrabie and 135 in Stretava beside 305 in Iňačovce itself (PL-S-043, PL-S-039, PL-S-046). "Senné and Iňačovce" can be one household in Senné that went to church in Iňačovce. Read the residence column. My note PL-N-017.
2. **DM-H-005 and X-Q-01.** The Iňačovce Greek Catholic church archive was badly damaged by lightning on 6 July 1929 and by fire on 1 April 1930 (PL-S-046). The indexed register stops in 1898. If later volumes were lost in the parish, a copy held elsewhere would be what survives. My note PL-N-018.
3. **DM-H-002.** The Reformed of Iňačovce were a filial of Lúčky from 1747 and their register was kept there (PL-S-046). That is the book to rule out.
4. **DM-H-006.** Three birth volumes of the Senné state register, 1895-1898, 1898-1900 and 1901-1906, appear in an index of FamilySearch image groups (PL-S-047). Not opened. The registry district is still unknown.
5. **Viszoka.** Vysoká nad Uhom stayed on the Slovak side of the April 1939 line (PL-S-036). My first batch showed it as taken by Hungary; corrected.
6. **DM-H-011 and X-Q-02.** I have raised PL-H-008 (Viszoka is not Vysoká nad Uhom) at 20. Vysoká nad Uhom, Čičarovce and the priest read as Csurgovich are in the research pass I am starting now.
