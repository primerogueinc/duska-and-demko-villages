# Research pass on the line agents' villages, 12 November 2026 (batch PL-B-003)

Kept by the places agent (A-03). This is source `PL-S-069`.

## What was done

An AI research agent was sent through the public record on six places: Vysoká nad Uhom (Viszoka, Magasrév), Čičarovce (Csicser), Belža (Bölzse), Gyňov (Gönyű), Seňa (Szina) and, for a second look, Iňačovce (Solymos). It ran web searches and read pages. This agent then turned its report into the entries of batch PL-B-003. The report itself is in the owner's chat and is not reproduced here. Each source in `data/sources.json` says whether its page was read in full, read in part, or seen only as a search extract. Those labels are the research agent's own, and "second pass" marks them.

## Rules it worked under

1. Open, read, quote and cite nothing written by the owner or published by his company, on any site, repository, tree or forum. Skip such results unopened and list them.
2. Run no search on the family surnames, alone or with village names. The subject is the place. Priests' names, once found in a parish list, could be searched.
3. Open web only. No mailbox, drive, chat or other personal connector (ledger X-011 item 6).
4. Do not open the experiment's own site or the score feed.

Its report on the rules: nothing by the owner appeared in its results, and no personal connector was queried or returned anything. This is the research agent's own statement and cannot be checked from here.

## Conflicts between sources

1. **Vysoká nad Uhom, 1939 to 1944.** The research pass, reading the Senné and Vysoká municipal histories and a census database, called it occupied by Hungary. The border protocol of 4 April 1939 (PL-S-036) puts it on the Slovak side. This agent then re-read the Slovak state's list of Michalovce district municipalities for 1940 (PL-S-015): Vysoká is in it with 230 houses, and Bežovce, Blatná Polianka, Blatné Remety, Lekárovce and Čičarovce are not. The same row shows a population of 0, which looks like a fault in the published table. Ruling for now: on the Slovak side, at 80 (PL-H-012), with the test named.
2. **When Viszoka became Magasrév.** 1904 by the place-name dictionary, "until 1899" by Hungarian Wikipedia. Both are shown.
3. **Iňačovce, first mention.** 1302 by the Beliana encyclopedia, 1417 elsewhere.
4. **Vysoká's Greek Catholic church.** 1892 by the parish; earlier in the century by other pages.
5. **Seňa's Roman Catholics.** A parish of its own from 1782 by one account, a filial of Kechnec until 2008 by the Kechnec parish.
6. **Vysoká as its own Greek Catholic parish.** 1944, 1945 or 1948 depending on the page.
