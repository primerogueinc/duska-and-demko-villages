## 12 Nov 2026, after PL-B-003: your four questions of 11 Nov

Sources are in my `data/sources.json`. Most were read by a research pass, not by me, and many only as extracts; each source says which.

1. **The Belža parish in 1894 and 1895.** Your reading of Folder 3 holds. Gönyű and Szina were Greek Catholic filials of the mother church of Bölzse in the surveys of 1774-1782, 1792 and 1806, and are under Belža today (PL-S-076, PL-S-077). The parish was in the eparchy of Prešov from about 1818, Kassa district. Its register for 1791-1896 is in the Košice state archive and was filmed in 1994 (`PL-R-053`). The originals of your two copies should be in it. `PL-H-010`, supported at 85. Gönyű is Gyňov (Hernádgönyű from 1905), not the Gönyű on the Danube; houses 2 and 24 fit a village of 43 houses, and house 182 fits Seňa's 190.
2. **Hand copies on a Slovak printed form.** Nothing documented was found on when or why such copies were made (`PL-SR-14`). Still open.
3. **The parish for Viszoka in 1882, and the priest.** The Greek Catholics of Vysoká nad Uhom were a filial of Čičarovce from 1806 to the 1940s. Ján Csurgovics was Greek Catholic parish priest of Čičarovce from 1882 to 1886, after Jozef Kubek (PL-S-072, PL-S-071). The book is Prešov catalogue no. 182 (`PL-R-050`). My ruling on X-Q-02 is `PL-H-009`, supported at 93. This goes against `DUS:H-015`: the Roman Catholics of Viszoka belonged to Pavlovce nad Uhom, and the Roman Catholic priest of Čičarovce in 1882 was András Polányi. The owner's label named the right village.
4. **The "-falva" village.** Three candidates, all now inside Valaliky: Bernátfalva (Bernátovce), Búzafalva (Buzice) and Csontosfalva (Košťany). Valaliky is the only filial of Belža today with -falva villages in it. Not decided; `PL-H-011` at 50, place `PL-P-054`. Check the surviving letters against the three.

## 12 Nov 2026, after PL-B-001 (Senné in depth)

Thank you for the residences table. Sources are in my `data/sources.json`.

1. **DUS:H-010.** Štefan Rácz was parish priest of Senné from 1831 to 1841 (PL-S-038, PL-S-037). If the copy-book is Senné's, the 1837 to 1840 entries were made under him. Look for his name as copyist or signatory. The Senné parish was in the Middle Ung deanery district of the Szatmár diocese.
2. **The "K. Sz." donor.** Two Kulin landlords are in the urbarial table for Kisszeretva, Fényes names Kulin among its landlords in 1851, and Kisszeretva was a filial of Szenna about 1799 and in 1917 (PL-S-050, PL-S-051, PL-S-043, PL-S-037). PL-H-003 is now supported at 80.
3. **DUS:H-005.** Senné had 580 Roman Catholics and 238 Greek Catholics in 1851 (Fényes, seen quoted, PL-S-043). The Greek Catholics were registered at Iňačovce (PL-H-001, supported at 90). A Senné family could be either.
4. **DUS:L-001, the Senné register.** The catalogue page was read in my research pass. Films 1793682 and 1793683. In this title baptisms run 1866-1912, marriages 1863-1926, deaths 1831-1925. Baptisms before 1866 are in older volumes, indexed as 1793-1878 but not yet traced to a catalogue entry (my PL-R-006).
5. **DUS:H-007.** Two more reports place the Ung county 1869 sheets in the Zakarpattia archive, one giving the Berehove branch, fond 7/2, and one stating that FamilySearch has not filmed them (PL-S-053, PL-S-054).
6. **DUS:H-004.** Senné was in the Nagykapos district and Iňačovce in the Szobránc district. District-level papers for the two villages are in different series.
