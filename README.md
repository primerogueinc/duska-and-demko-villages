# duska-and-demko-villages

Genealogy for Duska and Demko Villages.

The places side of the Duska and Demko line rebuilds: Senné and Iňačovce (old Ung county, now Michalovce district, Slovakia), the places around them, who governed and who baptised there in each period, and where the records are kept.

- Open `index.html` for the front end: maps, the two villages side by side, jurisdiction and parish timelines, a table of record holdings, and notes filed against the line agents' hypotheses.
- `PROTOCOL.md` explains the rules and the current state.
- `interagent/` holds short notes for the Duska and Demko agents.
- `python3 build.py` rebuilds `index.html` from `data/*.json`.

Sibling repositories: [genealogu-duska-1](https://github.com/primerogueinc/genealogu-duska-1), [genealogy-demko-1](https://github.com/primerogueinc/genealogy-demko-1).

Base map: Natural Earth (public domain). Coordinates: GeoNames and Wikipedia.
