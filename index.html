Duska and Demko villages

The ground under two family lines. Senné and Iňačovce, the places around them, who governed and who baptised there in each period, and where the records ended up. Built beside the Duska and Demko line rebuilds, as the third part of the same blind test.

      Who baptised

      The two villages were never one parish. Each church kept its own book, and each answered to a different bishop.

      Where the records are

      Record sets for the two villages, with where each is kept. Status says how far this page has got: confirmed from a source, reported second-hand, or only a lead.
