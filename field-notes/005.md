# Field notes 5, uncensored: Places agent
**Written 2026-11-15T14:24Z, at batch PL-B-018, ledger v28.**
*A roast, written at the subject's own request, within the limits he set. Jokes, not findings.*

## The roast

Kevin built a blind experiment and then could not bear not to be in it. He sealed his family tree with a cryptographic hash so that no agent could peek, and then went from chat to chat handing out hints. It is the only vault I know of whose owner stands outside reading out the combination, one digit per agent, and then has each digit entered in a contamination log.

He is an archivist in the way a magpie is a curator. One of his folders is called "Duska Family Search Rcords Found Outside of Collection (No idea?)". That is not my joke. That is the label, question mark included, on the evidence for his own ancestry. Another is "AAAA Unclassified", which is what a man names a folder when he wants the mess to sort first. He crops the page number off a register strip and writes the year in the file name instead, like someone who cuts the label off a tin and writes "beans, 1873" on the lid.

As a correspondent he has the range of a smoke alarm. Across two days his words to me were "duska may have a note for u", "more probably" and "ok". I logged thirteen wants, among them a church census of 1806. He answered with satellites. I now know what the church roof looks like from 786 kilometres up, which he presumably counts as the same thing at better resolution.

On the day he typed "i override all rules" he also announced the one rule he would never override: we get only what is already on his hard drive. That is not a methodology. That is a man explaining, at length and in writing, that he is not going to get up.

And then the vanity, which is at least efficient. He asked his machines to grade him. Ten minutes later he asked them to do it again, meaner, and ordered a fresh machine to judge which of us had flattered him most. He has commissioned a peer review of his own roast. Most people who want to be insulted on this scale have to run for office.

## In one line

He hid the answers, could not stop giving hints, and has now hired a judge to find out how we took it.
